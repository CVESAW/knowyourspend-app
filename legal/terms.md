## 2. What KnowYourSpend does

KnowYourSpend reads bank statements and receipts that you choose, on your device, and estimates which
companies ultimately own the businesses you paid and which countries those companies are based in.

## 3. Estimates, not advice

- Ownership information comes from published sources (including Companies House, Wikidata, GLEIF and

  "Unverified". Ownership changes, and published sources can be wrong or out of date. Results are
  estimates and may contain errors.
- KnowYourSpend gives information only. It is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and you
  shouldn't rely on it as the only basis for any decision.
- Where the app says a company's parent is incorporated in a particular country or a listed
  jurisdiction, that is a statement about where the company is registered. It doesn't mean the company
  has done anything unlawful or avoids tax.
- If you think something is wrong, tell us at hello@knowyourspend.org or use "Report a mistake" in
  the app, and we'll look into it.
