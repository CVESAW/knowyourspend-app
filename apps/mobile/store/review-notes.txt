KnowYourSpend reads bank statements (PDF or CSV files the user chooses) and receipt photos on the device, and shows which countries own the shops the user paid. It moves no money, gives no financial or investment advice, and does not connect to any bank. No account is needed: every feature works without signing in. Signing in is optional (with a code sent by email) and only stores an encrypted copy of the user's data, which only their passphrase opens.

To try it without a statement: open the app and tap "Try the demo" on the home screen.

To try a statement: download https://knowyourspend.org/sample-statement.csv (made-up data) to Files, then tap + > Bank statement > Choose file, and pick it.

Network use: the shop-name look-up, on by default, sends the names of unfamiliar shops (filtered to exclude people's names; never amounts or dates) to our look-up service. It can be turned off in Settings > Shops we don't know > Look up their owners online > Never. Only if the user chooses: emailing themselves their results (percentages and well-known shop names, never amounts), an account (an encrypted copy of their data) and anonymous counts (off by default). Receipt text recognition runs on the device with Google ML Kit, which sends Google its own basic diagnostics.
